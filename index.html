Built

Sales, margin and stock dashboard

I’d already built the point-of-sale system a skincare retailer runs on. It captured cost at the moment of sale and a signed stock ledger, but reported only what sold, not what earned. So I built the reporting layer on top of the data model I’d designed.

The three best-selling products generate 17.2% of revenue and 6.0% of gross profit. The number-one seller returns $1,128; a serum doing half its revenue returns $2,469.

The DAX reproduces the exact profit function the POS app already uses, so the report and the till can never disagree. Header discounts are allocated down to line grain, because margin per product can’t be computed otherwise. Stock on hand is a running total of signed movements rather than a stored balance, which is what makes stock-on-hand at any historical date possible.

Power BI · DAX · star schema · cohort retention · 40 measures · 4 pages

Live

Import storefront with a seven-stage order tracker

Import orders were taken in a WhatsApp group and tracked on a spreadsheet. Shipping runs through a private consolidator, so there was no courier tracking number to give anyone. The team hand-answered “where is my order” all day.

I mapped the real fulfilment process and built the storefront around it: a custom WordPress child theme, hand-written page templates, and a PHP order tracker where a customer enters their order number and email and sees which of seven stages their goods have reached. Every off-the-shelf plugin assumes a courier API, and this business has no courier.

The tracker matches orders to stages by status name, not slug. WooCommerce silently truncates long custom status slugs, and matching on the slug would have failed quietly the first time a stage name ran long.

WooCommerce · PHP · custom child theme · Paystack · ~5,800 lines

In rollout

Point-of-sale, stock and receipting system

Sales were written in a notebook and stock counted by eye, so nobody knew what actually sold or which products made money after cost. I mapped the sale-to-receipt process, agreed the requirements, then built it as a phone-first app.

Every sale, stock movement and void commits as a single database transaction, so inventory cannot drift out of step with sales. Voids restore stock without deleting history, because the history is the audit trail. Line items snapshot cost and price at the moment of sale, so historical margin stays true after a price change.

Next.js · React · Postgres · PWA · 9 screens

Design

Customer-service agent architecture

Every enquiry landed in one inbox: “how do I start a business” beside “where is my order”, and each got a hand-typed reply whose quality depended on who answered.

I built the classification model behind an automated response: nine intents, each with recognition patterns, a defined next action and a goal. Sixty-three documented workflow branches, plus escalation points and policy rules the agent cannot break: no profit promises, no invented shipping dates.

Intent taxonomy · workflow routing · policy design · specification only, not deployed
